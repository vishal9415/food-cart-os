## 1. The service
Food Cart OS provides food-cart owners with a mobile management application and provides customers with browser-based QR ordering pages. The owner is responsible for the cart, its menu, prices, food preparation and fulfilment.

## 2. Owner accounts
Owners must provide accurate account information, protect their sign-in credentials and keep staff access appropriate. Owners are responsible for activity performed by people they invite to their cart.

## 3. Menu and availability
Owners are responsible for menu descriptions, prices, item availability, taxes and preparation estimates. An owner may pause order acceptance. A customer can place an order only when the cart is active and accepting orders.

## 4. Orders and payment
The current version supports pickup orders and cash payment at the cart. Food Cart OS does not collect online payments in this version. The customer is responsible for paying the cart owner at pickup. An order is not proof that cash has been collected.
