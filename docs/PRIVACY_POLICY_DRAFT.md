## Information collected

### Food-cart owners and staff
- Account email address and authentication data managed by Supabase Auth.
- Optional owner profile name.
- Cart name, public QR slug, menu, prices, availability, opening status and preparation estimate.
- Staff membership and role assignments.
- Order queue and sales-report data.

### Customers placing QR orders
- Customer name and phone number, both optional in the current checkout.
- Items ordered, quantities, order value, order time, order status and pickup token.
- A random browser session token used to recover recent orders for the same cart.

Food Cart OS currently supports cash payment at pickup only. It does not collect card numbers, UPI credentials or online payment details.

## How information is used
- Create and secure owner accounts.
- Display the correct cart menu from its QR code.
- Accept and process customer orders.
- Prevent duplicate checkout submissions and abusive checkout attempts.
- Show order status and recent orders to the customer who placed them.
- Provide cart owners with order management and sales summaries.
- Support customer service, troubleshooting and platform security.
